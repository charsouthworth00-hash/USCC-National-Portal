# United States Of CJS City (USCC)

**One Nation. 55 States. A Greater Tomorrow.**

The official national portal project for the **United States Of CJS City (USCC)** — a creative world-building project bringing the country's information, institutions, history and national resources together in one place.

> **Project status:** In development. This repository is the starting point for the USCC national website. Planned features are not presented as completed or official until they are built and confirmed.

## About USCC

- **Full name:** United States Of CJS City
- **Abbreviation:** USCC
- **Short name:** CJS City
- **Capital:** CJS City Headquarters
- **States:** 55
- **Government system:** Presidential system
- **President (project canon):** Charles Jonathon Jack Southworth I
- **Founder (project canon):** Cara James Smith
- **Motto:** “One Nation. 55 States. A Greater Tomorrow.”

## What this portal aims to include

- **Home** — a central landing page for the national portal.
- **About USCC** — an introduction to the country and its institutions.
- **Government** — a directory of confirmed national offices and officials.
- **55 States** — a state directory with individual state profiles and, when an approved map is available, an interactive map.
- **Constitution** — an archive for the approved Constitution and its amendments.
- **History** — a timeline of the country's recorded history.
- **National Calendar** — dates and calendar information following the project's established calendar rules.
- **National Symbols** — the flag and other confirmed national symbols.
- **Culture** — information about national culture and traditions.
- **Records and FAQs** — reference information and answers to common questions.

These are planned sections. Details that have not yet been confirmed will be marked as **To be confirmed** rather than invented.

## Getting started

The website is being developed as a browser-based project.

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `USCC_Official_Website.html` in a modern web browser.
3. As development progresses, check this README for updated setup and project instructions.

## Project principles

- Preserve the established USCC name, identity and project canon.
- Keep the website accessible and usable on desktop and mobile browsers.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed information from proposals and unfinished content.
- Do not invent state capitals, officials, constitutional articles, national symbols or other facts that have not been approved.

## Contributing

This is a developing creative project. Changes should keep the national portal consistent, clear and easy to use. Please describe significant changes and identify any proposed lore or details that still need confirmation.
